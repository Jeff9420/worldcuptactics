- Add or verify search-intent answer, tactical depth, current facts, FAQ, sources, and internal links.
- Keep the article specific enough to avoid thin SEO content.
- Run lint, production build, local page smoke, then commit and deploy.

## Quality checklist

- The first two paragraphs answer the main search query directly.
- The article includes concrete match/team/context facts, not generic football language.
- Tactical claims explain mechanism: shape, trigger, space, player role, or trade-off.
- The page has useful internal links to 2026 hubs, match pages, team pages, or archive matches.
- FAQ answers are short and eligible for structured data.
- Source links are official or clearly authoritative when facts may change.
